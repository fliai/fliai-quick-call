Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: FLiAI Quick Call works only on your local network. It doesn't collect, sell or send your data to us or to any server, and it has no analytics or tracking.

What the app stores on your computer

  • A random identifier, created on first launch, so teammates see you as the same person each time.
  • The display name you choose (by default, your computer's user name).
  • Your status: Available or Busy.

These are saved in a small settings file on your own computer and never leave it except as described below.

What is shared, and with whom

To let teammates find and call you, the app shares your display name, status, random identifier and local network address with other computers running FLiAI Quick Call on the same local network (for example, your office Wi-Fi). Call requests and answers (“Coming”, “Busy”, “No answer”) travel directly between those computers.

Nothing is sent to the internet. There is no account, no central server and no cloud storage.

What we don't collect

  • No audio, video, messages, contacts, files or location.
  • No analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or tracking.
  • We, the developers, receive no data from the app at all.

Downloads and updates

If you install from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles downloads and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. If you install with our install script, your computer downloads the app from our download server, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for operating the service.

Removing your data

Uninstall the app to stop sharing. To remove the settings file as well, delete the app's settings folder (lk.fliai.quickcall) in your user application data. Teammates' computers forget your details when their app restarts.

Children

The app is a workplace tool and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
