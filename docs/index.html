Features

Small, quiet, and right where you look.

Everything you need to get someone's attention, and nothing you don't.

Finds teammates by itself

Anyone running Quick Call on the same network appears automatically. Nothing to configure, no invite codes.

Lives at the top of your screen

A slim island that hugs the top edge, or the notch on newer MacBooks. Hover to open it, move away to hide it.

Busy means do not disturb

Set yourself to Busy and calls don't ring at all. The caller is told straight away, so nobody is left waiting.

Impossible to miss

An incoming call glows around every screen you have, even over full-screen apps, with a soft chime every few seconds.

One-tap answers

Reply “Coming” or “Busy”. If nobody answers in 30 seconds, the caller sees “No answer”.

Private by design

No cloud, no account, no tracking. Computers talk directly to each other on your local network, and nothing leaves it.

How it works

Three steps. No setup.

Install it

Paste one line into Terminal or PowerShell. It starts right away, and again whenever you log in.

Hover the top of your screen

The island opens with everyone on your network, showing who's available, busy or offline.

Press Call

Their screen lights up. You'll see “Coming”, “Busy” or “No answer” a moment later.
